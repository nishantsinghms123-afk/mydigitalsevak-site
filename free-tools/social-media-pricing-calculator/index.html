How much should you charge for social media management in India?

A starter social media retainer (about 12 posts a month on one platform, with community replies and a monthly report) typically runs ₹15,000–₹25,000/month in metro cities, ₹10,000–₹18,000/month in large tier-2 cities and ₹6,000–₹12,000/month in smaller cities. Reels, shoots, extra platforms and ads management are priced on top. Full-service retainers commonly run ₹50,000–₹4,00,000/month in metros. These are indicative market bands from our city-by-city agency guides, not quotes.

How to use the calculator

  1. Pick the client's city tier. Pricing in India varies a lot by market. The same package sells for very different amounts in Mumbai and in Agra.
  2. Tick the platforms you'll actually run. The first is included, and each extra platform adds adaptation and community work.
  3. Enter the monthly deliverables: static posts, carousels, reels and half-day shoots.
  4. Add ads management if you'll run the client's campaigns, and enter their monthly ad spend.
  5. Read the range, not just the midpoint. Go towards the low end for a new agency or a small local business. Go towards the high end when you bring strong creative, fast turnaround and reporting the client can read.

How the estimate is calculated

We'd rather show the method than hide it. Every number comes from the price bands already published on this site:

  • Static post = the tier's starter retainer band ÷ 12, because a starter retainer is roughly 12 posts a month on one platform (our pricing guide). In a metro that's ₹15,000–₹25,000/month ÷ 12.
  • Carousel = 1.5 × a static post (more slides to design and write). Reel = 3 × a static post (scripting, editing and sound). These multipliers are our working assumptions, not survey data.
  • Extra platforms: each platform after the first adds 20% of the content cost, for resizing, caption adaptation and community management.
  • Half-day shoot = 1.25 × the tier's starter band. In a smaller city that tops out at the ₹15,000 half-day example in our retainer vs project pricing guide.
  • Ads management = a flat ₹10,000–₹25,000, or 15–20% of ad spend once that is higher, as described in our pricing guide.
  • Strategy, community replies and a monthly report are treated as included. Totals are rounded to the nearest ₹500 and exclude GST.

These are indicative ranges for planning a quote, not market research or a guarantee of what a client will pay.

Turning the number into a proposal

Quote packages, not per-platform prices. Clients will cut a platform to cut the price. State your fee plus GST, and list the deliverables and revision rounds, so scope creep has something to push against. Frequently asked questions

How much does social media management cost per month in India?

Starter retainers of about 12 posts a month on one platform typically run ₹15,000–₹25,000/month in metro cities, ₹10,000–₹18,000/month in large tier-2 cities and ₹6,000–₹12,000/month in smaller cities. Full-service retainers with content, ads, influencer work and reporting run much higher, commonly ₹50,000–₹4,00,000/month in metros. Reels, shoots and extra platforms push the price up.

Should ad spend be included in my social media retainer?

No. Keep ad spend and your management fee as separate lines. The client should pay Meta or Google directly, and you charge a management fee, commonly a flat ₹10,000–₹25,000 or 15–20% of spend at larger budgets.

Should I quote social media prices with or without GST?

Quote your fee exclusive of GST and add 18% on the invoice if you are GST-registered. A GST-inclusive quote silently takes 18% out of your margin. Registered business clients can usually claim the GST back as input tax credit.

Why does a reel cost more than a static post?

A reel needs a concept, a script or shot list, footage, editing, sound and captions, so it takes several times the hours of a static design. This calculator prices a reel at three static posts, which is a working assumption you should adjust to your own production time.

Are these prices guaranteed market rates?

No. They are indicative ranges built from the price bands published in our city guides and pricing guide, using the stated multipliers. Your experience, niche, creative quality and the client's budget will move the real number.
