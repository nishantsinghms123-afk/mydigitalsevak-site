Indian Festival Marketing Calendar 2027: Dates & Lead Times

By Nishant Singh, founder of My Digital Sevak — written after running a social media agency across 63+ brands · Published October 17, 2026

Diwali 2027 falls on Friday 29 October, ten days earlier than in 2026. Holi is on 22 March, Raksha Bandhan on 17 August, Ganesh Chaturthi on 4 September and Navratri starts on 30 September. Brief clients 10–12 weeks before Diwali and Navratri, 4–6 weeks before mid-sized festivals, and batch the simple greetings once a quarter.

Around this time every year, our clients start asking for next year's plan: the retainer renewal, the Q1 budget, "what are we doing for Holi?" A festival calendar is usually the first page of that plan. The trouble is that most 2027 lists online are copied holiday tables with no dates checked and nothing to tell you when the work has to start.

This is the calendar we use for our own clients. We checked every Hindu festival date against Drik Panchang's 2027 monthly pages for New Delhi, and we've added the lead time each festival needs. Regional calendars sometimes put a festival a day earlier or later, so confirm the date with the client before you schedule the post.

How far ahead should brands plan each festival?

A list of dates doesn't tell you when to start. A Diwali campaign needs the offer locked, a shoot done, and approvals collected, all before the first teaser goes out. So we group festivals into three tiers and work backwards from each date:

TierFestivalsSend the briefShoot and designFirst post goes live
1. Sales seasonNavratri, Dussehra, Dhanteras, Diwali10–12 weeks before6–8 weeks before3–4 weeks before
2. Category peaksHoli, Eid, Akshaya Tritiya, Raksha Bandhan, Ganesh Chaturthi, Onam, Karwa Chauth, Christmas5–6 weeks before3–4 weeks before7–10 days before
3. GreetingsLohri, Republic Day, Vasant Panchami, Baisakhi, Rama Navami, Janmashtami, Guru Nanak JayantiOnce a quarter, in one batch2 weeks beforeOn the day

Two things push those lead times earlier.

  • Shipping. Any brand that ships products, especially Rakhi and Diwali gifts sent to other cities, has to post its last order date a week before the festival and promote the range before that.
  • When people decide what to buy. Online festive sales start weeks before Diwali, not on the day. In 2025, Redseer reported about ₹25,000 crore of online sales (GMV) in the first two days of the festive sales alone, 23–25% more than the year before (Deccan Chronicle). Redseer's 2025 forecast covered the 30–35 days before Diwali (Alchemist Pro). If a brand's first Diwali post goes live in Diwali week, most shoppers have already bought somewhere else.

What's different about 2027 for marketers?

If you copy your 2026 plan and just change the year, you will get the timing wrong. Here's what changes:

  1. The festive season moves forward by ten days. Diwali moves from 8 November 2026 to 29 October 2027, and Navratri starts on 30 September. So Diwali briefs go out in early August, not September.
  2. Pitru Paksha (16–29 September) falls right before Navratri. Many Hindu families avoid big purchases during those two weeks. Use the time for teasers, wishlists and pre-bookings, and save launches and the big offers for Navratri.
  3. Akshaya Tritiya and Mother's Day are both on Sunday 9 May. Jewellery clients have to choose a main message or plan two separate posts. Decide this in March, not the week before.
  4. Holi is a long weekend. Holika Dahan is on Sunday and Holi on Monday, so travel, hotels and restaurants can sell a three-day break from Saturday 20 March.
  5. Diwali also falls on a long weekend. Lakshmi Puja is Friday and Bhai Dooj is Sunday. Schedule the greeting posts in advance and keep a small team on DMs for Dhanteras, the Wednesday before.
On Women's Day (8 March): a pink discount banner usually gets more angry comments than sales. If the client has nothing real to say, like women on the team, women founders they stock, or a cause they actually fund, a short greeting is safer than a campaign.

How do agencies turn this calendar into client plans?

Don't send every client all 32 dates. Here's how we turn the calendar into a plan for each client:

  • Pick six to ten festivals per client. A jeweller in Surat needs Akshaya Tritiya, Navratri, Dhanteras, Karwa Chauth and the wedding season. A Kochi café needs Onam, Vishu and Christmas. Our city pages for agencies in Kolkata and Surat show how much priorities differ by region.
  • Map the audience, not the agency's own calendar. A brand whose customers are in Tamil Nadu wishes people for Pongal and Puthandu, not Lohri. Ask the client which festivals their customers celebrate. It takes one WhatsApp message.
  • Price festive extra work separately. Extra Diwali reels, a festive shoot and boost budgets are not part of a normal retainer. Agree on them in writing early. Our add-on price list template covers how to price them.
  • Collect every festive approval in one batch. Send all Tier 3 greetings to every client for approval in one week each quarter, then schedule them so nobody has to post on the festival day itself. See how to bulk-schedule a month of content.
  • Write a festival-specific playbook for the big ones. Our Diwali post ideas for brands shows the level of detail Tier 1 festivals need, with ideas for each industry and a day-by-day countdown.

Frequently asked questions

When is Diwali in 2027?

Lakshmi Puja (Diwali) falls on Friday 29 October 2027. Drik Panchang lists Dhanteras on Wednesday 27 October, Govardhan Puja on Saturday 30 October and Bhai Dooj on Sunday 31 October for New Delhi. That is ten days earlier than Diwali 2026, so the whole festive season moves forward.

Are the 2027 festival dates final?

Hindu festival dates come from the panchang and are known well in advance, but regional calendars can differ by a day. Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha depend on moon sighting, so treat those dates as tentative. Plan with these dates now and reconfirm each one with the client about a month before it.

How many festivals should a brand post for in a year?

Most brands should treat six to ten festivals properly and skip the rest. Pick the ones their customers actually celebrate and the ones that drive sales in their category. A greeting for every festival on the calendar fills the feed with posts nobody remembers and eats the design time the big festivals need.

When should agencies start planning Diwali 2027 content?

Send the Diwali brief to clients by early August 2027 and lock offers and shoots in early September. Navratri starts on 30 September and Pitru Paksha runs from 16 to 29 September, so October is too late to start. Teasers should go live around the first week of October.
