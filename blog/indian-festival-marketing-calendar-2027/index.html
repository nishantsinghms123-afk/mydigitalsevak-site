Indian Festival Marketing Calendar 2027: Dates and Lead Times

By Nishant Singh, founder of My Digital Sevak — written after running a social media agency across 63+ brands · Published 22 Nov 2026

India's 2027 festival marketing calendar peaks in October: Dussehra on 9 Oct, Karwa Chauth on 18 Oct and Diwali on 29 Oct. The earlier anchors are Eid-ul-Fitr (10 Mar), Holi (23 Mar), Raksha Bandhan (17 Aug) and Ganesh Chaturthi (4 Sep). Brief Diwali content six to ten weeks ahead, and most other festivals three to four weeks ahead.

Most "festival calendar 2027" pages are built for devotees: every vrat, every ekadashi, every muhurat. That's useful at home and useless in a planning meeting. A brand doesn't need 200 dates. It needs the 25 or so that move sales or deserve a post, plus a deadline for when the creative has to be ready.

So that's what this is. The dates below come from one source we trust for planning: the central government's 2027 holiday list. That's the DoPT order dated 16 July 2026, with its gazetted and restricted holiday annexures. We checked a few against Drik Panchang (Raksha Bandhan 17 Aug and Karwa Chauth 18 Oct agree). Where calendars disagree, we say so.

How early should brands plan festival marketing?

Earlier than the client thinks, and later than the agency fears. In our experience, the lead time isn't set by the festival but by the slowest thing in the chain: a product shoot, a stock decision, a model booking, or a client who approves on WhatsApp once a week. This is the rule of thumb we plan with:

Which festivals should a brand skip?

Not every holiday is a marketing moment, and getting this wrong costs more than skipping a post. Three rules we follow:

  • Solemn days are not sale days. Muharram (16 Jun in the government list) and Good Friday (26 Mar) are days of mourning for many people. No "Happy Good Friday", no offers. If the brand posts at all, it's a quiet, respectful message, and usually nothing is better.
  • Region beats nation. A Kochi jeweller's year turns on Onam; a Patna sweet shop's turns on Chhath; a Pune developer's turns on Gudi Padwa. National festival lists underweight all three. Start from where the client's customers live. Our Kochi and Patna pages list the local agency scene if you need regional help.
  • Greeting spam fades into the feed. Twelve "Happy X" posts a year with the logo in the corner do nothing. Pick the four or five festivals that matter to the business and do real content for them: an offer, a behind-the-scenes reel, a customer story. Send a short greeting for the rest, or skip them.

How to turn this into a client calendar

We do it the same way for every brand, in December for the next year:

  1. Block the revenue dates first. Mark the five or six festivals that matter to this client on the calendar, with the "brief by" date as a task, before anything else goes in.
  2. Ask the client two questions. Which festivals did you run offers for last year, and which day does your city celebrate Holi, Sankranti and Eid? Lunar dates vary, and the client knows their own customers.
  3. Lock team holidays. The festivals your clients sell on are often days your team takes off. Diwali week is the obvious clash. Schedule festival posts so they're made and approved before the team leaves.
  4. Fill the gaps last. Regular pillar content goes between the festival spikes. That's where an AI draft saves real time, as long as the festival dates are fixed by a human first. We wrote about that in our AI content calendar guide.

Frequently asked questions

When is Diwali in 2027?

Diwali falls on Friday, 29 October 2027, per the central government's 2027 holiday list. The five days run from Dhanteras on 27 October to Bhai Dooj on 31 October, with Govardhan Puja on 30 October. Brands selling gifts, gold or fashion should brief Diwali content by late August.

How early should brands plan festival marketing in India?

Six to ten weeks ahead for Diwali, Dhanteras and wedding-season pushes in jewellery, fashion and electronics, where shoots and stock decisions come first. Three to four weeks for most other festivals with an offer. One to two weeks for greeting posts. Paid budgets should be fixed before the festive auction gets expensive.

Are Indian festival dates for 2027 fixed?

Not all of them. Hindu festivals follow the lunar calendar and can differ by a day between regions and panchangs; Holi 2027 is listed as 23 March by the government but 22 March by some calendars. Eid dates depend on moon sighting. Confirm with the client's local practice before scheduling.
