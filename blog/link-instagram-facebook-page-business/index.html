How to Link an Instagram Account to a Facebook Page for Business: 3 Ways, and Fixes When It Fails

By Nishant Singh, founder of My Digital Sevak — written after running a social media agency across 63+ brands · Published 24 Nov 2026

Switch the Instagram account to a professional (Business) account first. Then, on Facebook, open the Page, go to Settings, then Linked accounts, choose Instagram and click Connect account. You can also do it from Instagram's Edit profile screen or from Meta Business Suite. You need full control or task access on the Page.

In our agency, this one step has held up more client launches than any design or caption problem. The calendar is approved and the reels are edited, and then on day one the scheduler says it can't find the brand's Instagram account. Nine times out of ten the cause is the same: the Instagram account was never linked to the brand's Facebook Page, or it's linked to a Page nobody on the team can manage.

Here is the linking process as it works in late 2026, the checklist to run first, and how we handle it for clients so nobody shares a password.

Why link Instagram to a Facebook Page at all?

For a brand posting by hand from the phone, the link is optional. It becomes necessary as soon as someone else, like an agency, a scheduling tool or an ad account, needs to work on the account.

  • Scheduling and auto-publishing tools. Meta's Instagram Platform overview lists more than one way for an app to connect. The long-standing one, Instagram API with Facebook Login, "serves Instagram professional accounts linked to a Facebook Page." Many schedulers use it, so no Page link means no connection.
  • Ads. Running Instagram ads for a brand is far simpler when the account sits with the Page in the brand's Meta business setup.
  • One inbox. Meta Business Suite shows Facebook and Instagram messages and comments together only for linked accounts.
  • Access without passwords. Once Instagram is tied to a Page, the client can give the agency access through Meta instead of sharing the Instagram password.

What do you need before you start?

Run this checklist first. Most failed attempts skip one of these:

  1. A professional Instagram account. Personal accounts can't be linked to a Page. Switch under Settings, then account type and tools. Choose Business rather than Creator if you'll use a scheduler: several tools, ours included, connect only Business accounts.
  2. A Facebook Page for the brand, not the owner's personal profile. Many small Indian businesses have run for years from a profile named after the shop. Create a Page first.
  3. The right access on that Page. Facebook's help centre says you can connect or disconnect if you have Facebook access or task access to the Page. In practice, use someone with full control.
  4. The Instagram login, on the phone of whoever has that Page access. This is the one moment the two need to meet.
  5. The same business portfolio, if the Page is managed in Meta Business Suite or Business Manager. Add the Instagram account to the same portfolio, or the link may not show where you expect it.
  6. One Page, one Instagram. A Page can be connected to only one Instagram account at a time, as Later's troubleshooting guide notes. If an old account is linked, disconnect it first.

How do you link Instagram to a Facebook Page? The 3 ways

All three create the same link. Pick whichever one the person with access can reach. Meta renames menus often, so the labels may differ slightly on your screen.

1. From the Facebook Page (desktop)

These are the steps in Facebook's help centre article on connecting Instagram and your Page:

  1. Log in to Facebook and click your profile photo at the top right.
  2. Click See all profiles and switch to the Page.
  3. Click the Page's profile photo, then Settings and privacy, then Settings.
  4. Under Permissions, click Linked accounts, then Instagram.
  5. Click Connect account, log in to Instagram when asked, and confirm.

2. From the Instagram app

  1. Open the brand's profile and tap Edit profile.
  2. Under Public business information, tap Page.
  3. Choose the existing Page from the list (it only shows Pages your linked Facebook account can manage), or create one.

This is the quickest route when the client's owner is sitting with you and has the Page on their own Facebook account.

3. From Meta Business Suite

Open Business Suite for the brand, go to Settings, and add or connect the Instagram account there. If the Page belongs to a business portfolio, this is the cleanest route, because the Instagram account ends up in the same portfolio as the Page and you can give your team access to both from one place.

Accounts Center, Page link, business portfolio: what's the difference?

This is the confusion behind most "but I already connected it" conversations. Three different Meta links exist, and a scheduler usually needs the second:

LinkWhat it connectsWhere you set itWhat it's for
Accounts CenterInstagram ↔ a personal Facebook profileInstagram or Facebook settings, Accounts CenterShared login, sharing your own posts across apps
Page linkInstagram ↔ the brand's Facebook PagePage settings → Linked accounts, or Instagram Edit profile → PageTools using Facebook Login, shared inbox, ads
Business portfolioInstagram and Page as assets of one businessMeta Business Suite settingsGiving staff and agencies access without passwords

A client who says "Instagram is connected to Facebook" usually means Accounts Center. Ask them to check the Page's Linked accounts screen instead.

Connected, but publishing failsThe Page needs Page Publishing AuthorizationComplete it from the Page's notifications or settings

The Page Publishing Authorization row is real but rarely mentioned. Meta's content publishing documentation says an Instagram account "connected to a Page that requires Page Publishing Authorization cannot be published to until PPA has been completed", and that an app can't tell whether a Page needs it. So Meta advises completing it in advance. If posts fail with no clear error, check this first. Our guide to failed Instagram posts covers the other causes.

How agencies should do this for clients

The worst version of this step is the common one: the client WhatsApps their Instagram password and Facebook OTP to a junior executive at 9 PM. Here's the version we use during onboarding instead:

  1. Do it on the kickoff call. A 15-minute screen share with the client's owner, using their phone and their Facebook account. They make every change themselves; you tell them what to tap.
  2. The client stays the owner. The Page, the Instagram account and the business portfolio belong to the brand. The agency gets access as a partner or as named people, never by sharing logins. Our no-password auto-posting guide explains why this matters when staff leave.
  3. Clean up old links. Check whether a previous agency or freelancer still has Page access, and remove them while the owner is there.
  4. Connect the tool the same day, and do one test post before the calendar starts.
  5. Write it on the onboarding checklist, with the date and who has access, as covered in our client onboarding guide.

One warning before you unlink anything to "start fresh": Facebook's help centre says disconnecting Instagram from a Page may cancel an active Meta Verified subscription in some cases. If the client pays for Meta Verified, check before you unlink. Our Meta Verified guide covers what that subscription includes.

Do you still need the Page link if a tool offers "Instagram login"?

Meta's developer overview also lists Instagram API with Instagram Login, which "serves Instagram professional accounts without requiring a linked Facebook Page". It also lists a newer Unified Login, which needs a business portfolio instead. Some tools offer an "Instagram only" button for this reason. The trade-off: according to that same overview, the Instagram Login route doesn't support hashtag search or product tagging, and you still lose the shared inbox and easy ads setup.

Our own Instagram connection in My Digital Sevak uses Facebook Login, so each brand's Instagram must be a Business account linked to a Page your team can manage. We think that's the right default for agencies anyway: a brand with a linked Page and a proper business portfolio is a brand you can hand over, staff up or offboard cleanly. If you're setting this up for a technical integration, our Instagram API access guide goes deeper.

Whether you run an agency in Dehradun or Lucknow, put this step in week one of every new client. It takes 15 minutes, and if you skip it you can lose the first week of posts.

Frequently asked questions

Can I link a personal Instagram account to a Facebook Page?

No. Only professional Instagram accounts can be linked to a Facebook Page. Switching is free and takes a minute in the Instagram app under account type and tools. For a brand that will use a scheduling tool, choose Business rather than Creator, because several tools only connect Business accounts.

Is adding Instagram to Accounts Center the same as linking it to a Page?

No. Accounts Center links your Instagram account to your personal Facebook profile, for shared login and cross-posting from your own profile. A scheduling tool that uses Facebook Login needs the Instagram account linked to the business's Facebook Page, which is done from the Page's linked accounts setting or from Instagram's edit profile screen.

Will unlinking Instagram from my Facebook Page delete anything?

Your posts and followers stay on both accounts. What stops is everything that depends on the link: scheduling tools connected through the Page, the shared inbox, and ads that use the Instagram account. Facebook's help centre also warns that disconnecting can cancel an active Meta Verified subscription in some cases, so check before you unlink.
