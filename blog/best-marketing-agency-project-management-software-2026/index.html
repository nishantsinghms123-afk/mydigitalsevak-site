Best Marketing Agency Project Management Software in 2026 (Ranked)

By Nishant Singh, founder of My Digital Sevak — written after running a social media agency across 63+ brands · Published July 10, 2026 · Updated 10 Nov 2026

The best marketing agency project management software in 2026 depends on your agency type: My Digital Sevak for Indian social-media agencies that want team tasks, timesheets, client approvals, publishing and GST billing in one place; Scoro or Productive for large full-service agencies that live on utilisation and profitability reports; Bonsai for freelancer-led studios; and ClickUp, Asana or Monday if you only need task tracking and want the biggest integration ecosystems.

I run an agency. Before building our own software, we tried most of the tools on this list with real client work — so this comparison is opinionated, and yes, our own product is on it (first, with the reasoning stated plainly so you can discount my bias). Every other pick is a tool I'd genuinely recommend for the agency type named.

1. My Digital Sevak — best for Indian social media agencies

My Digital Sevak

₹999–2,499/month flat (whole team) · 7-day money-back guarantee · built in India

Full disclosure: this is our product. It exists because nothing on this list handles the actual daily loop of a social agency — plan content, get client approval, publish to Instagram, report, invoice with GST. My Digital Sevak does all five in one workspace, and since this post first went up it has also grown a full team workspace: tasks in List, Board, Calendar, Table, Gantt and Workload views, with subtasks, checklists, dependencies, custom fields, automations and webhooks; WhatsApp reminders before a task is due; team chat with screen and voice clips; docs, wiki and forms; and timesheets whose billable hours drop straight onto the client's GST invoice. What it doesn't have: the integration marketplaces and native iOS/Android team apps that ClickUp, Asana and Monday have built over many years, or Scoro-grade utilisation and profitability analytics for a 50-person firm. Some features are plan-gated too — automations, WhatsApp reminders and timesheets start on Growth (₹1,499/month); goals, dashboards, custom roles and 2FA on Agency (₹2,499/month). The full list is on the features page.

Choose it if you're a social-first agency in India and want your team's tasks and your clients' approvals, posts and invoices in the same system. Skip it if you're a 50-person full-service firm needing deep resource utilisation reporting — that's Scoro's territory — or if your workflow depends on a long list of third-party integrations.

2. Scoro — best for large full-service agencies

From $17/user/month (5-user minimum) · quoting, retainers, utilization

Scoro is the most complete "agency operating system" for firms of 20+ people billing by the hour: quoting, budgets, utilization and financial reporting are genuinely excellent. It assumes a Western workflow — no GST, no UPI, and clients interact by email, not an app.

Choose it for hourly-billing, multi-department agencies with ops staff to run it.

3. Productive — best for tracking agency profitability

~$9–10/user/month (3-seat minimum) · budgets, margins, resourcing

Productive connects project delivery to margins better than anything near its price — you see which client is profitable in real time. Like Scoro, it stops at delivery: no approvals, no publishing, no client-facing app.

Choose it if per-project profitability is your top question every month.

4. Bonsai — best for freelancers and tiny studios

~$9/user/month · proposals, contracts, invoices

Bonsai wraps proposals, contracts, light project tracking and invoicing into one friendly tool. It's ideal at 1–3 people; agencies outgrow its project depth quickly, and invoicing isn't GST-aware.

Choose it as a solo operator; move on when a team joins.

5–8. ClickUp, Asana, Teamwork, Monday — best for pure task tracking

Free–$10.99/user/month · tasks, boards, docs

These four are excellent generic project tools with free tiers, mature mobile apps and integration libraries far bigger than any agency-specific tool's — ours included. On the internal side they're strong: all four give you several task views, and all four track time, though Asana keeps native time tracking for Advanced, Monday for Pro, and ClickUp puts timesheets on Business. The catch for agencies: they only see the internal half of the work. Client approvals happen over screenshots, publishing is manual (or another subscription), reporting is another tool again, and only Teamwork raises invoices — from logged time, on its Accelerate plan, and not GST-aware. If your agency's work products are social content and campaigns, you'll be paying for 3–4 more tools around them — the math we break down on the agency management software page.

Choose one if you only need task boards, rely on lots of third-party integrations, and already have systems for approvals, publishing, reporting and billing.

How to choose (60-second version)

  • Social-first agency in India → My Digital Sevak (team tasks, approvals, publishing, timesheets and GST billing in one login)
  • 20+ people, hourly billing, multi-department, utilisation reporting → Scoro or Productive
  • Solo/freelance → Bonsai
  • Just need task boards, with lots of integrations → ClickUp or Asana free tier
  • Retainer/client-services with time tracking → Teamwork outside India; in India, My Digital Sevak if you want billable hours to land on a GST invoice without re-typing them

Whichever you pick, insist on one thing: the tool should reduce the number of places work lives, not add another tab. An agency's real cost isn't the subscription — it's the handoffs between tools.
