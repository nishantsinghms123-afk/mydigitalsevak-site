Is Meta Verified for Business Worth It in India? An Agency's Honest Answer

By Nishant Singh, founder of My Digital Sevak — written after running a social media agency across 63+ brands · Published 23 Nov 2026

Meta Verified for business is worth it in India for brands that get impersonated, sell through DMs or WhatsApp, or need a human at Meta when an account breaks. It starts at ₹639 a month. It is not worth it as a growth tactic: Meta promises better search placement, not more reach in the feed or Reels.

Every few weeks a client forwards me a screenshot of a competitor's profile with a blue tick and asks, "Humein bhi chahiye. Kitna lagega?" The honest answer has two parts. The price is easy. The harder part is explaining what the tick buys, because most business owners assume it means more people will see their posts. It doesn't, and an agency that sells it that way will be explaining flat reach two months later.

This guide is the conversation I now have with clients: what Meta Verified for business includes, what Meta's newer Meta One plans add, what neither does, and a simple test for whether a brand should pay.

What is Meta Verified for business?

It's a paid subscription that puts a verified badge on a business's Facebook Page or Instagram account after Meta checks the business is real. Meta launched the business plans in India in July 2024 in four tiers, Standard, Plus, Premium and Max. All of them include the same core: the badge, account support and impersonation protection. Higher tiers add faster support (chat or email, then a call-back, then case monitoring) and more links in Reels. At launch, prices started at ₹639 a month for one app and went up to ₹21,000 a month for two apps on a 12-month introductory offer, and India purchases were possible only from the iOS or Android app.

Getting it isn't automatic. Meta's original business announcement lists the conditions: the business account and the Page or Instagram account must have existed for a minimum time, show a minimum level of activity, and have two-factor authentication turned on. A Page created last month with six posts won't qualify, whatever the client is willing to pay.

What changed in 2026 with Meta One?

In September 2026 Meta rolled out Meta One, a wider subscription family that includes four business plans. Business Today reported the India prices as follows:

First, Meta told existing subscribers that "nothing changes right now" for current Meta Verified plans and that it will notify them when they can switch. Second, these are "from" prices reported at launch. Open the professional dashboard on the client's own account and use the price it shows on the day.

Does Meta Verified increase reach?

This is the question that decides most purchases, so be precise. Meta's 2023 announcement says verified businesses are featured, "including appearing at or near the top of search results". That's search: someone typing your brand name into Instagram finds the real account first. Nothing in Meta's announcements of these plans promises more reach in the feed, in Reels or on Explore, and nothing says ads get cheaper.

In practice, the brands I've seen buy the badge kept the same reach they had the month before. Reach still comes from what you post, how often, and whether people stop scrolling for it. If a client's reach has dropped, the badge won't fix it; their content mix and posting consistency will. Who should pay for it? A simple test

I recommend it when at least two of these are true:

  1. Fakes exist. Someone has already made a copycat Page or a "customer care" account using the brand's name and logo. In India this is common for jewellers, travel companies, hospitals and anything that takes advance payments. Impersonation protection and the badge both help customers tell the real account from the fake.
  2. Sales happen in DMs or on WhatsApp. If customers pay after a chat, trust at the moment of payment is worth money. The verified WhatsApp Business channel in Meta One Essential matters here too.
  3. The account is a real business asset. If a hacked or disabled account would stop the business for a week, the support access alone justifies ₹639 a month. Anyone who has tried to recover a hacked business Page through the standard help forms knows why.
  4. The brand is searched by name. Restaurants, clinics, schools and D2C brands that get searched after seeing an ad or a hoarding benefit from appearing first in search.

Skip it, or wait, when the account is new, when nobody searches for the brand yet, or when the client's real hope is "more followers". Spend the money on one more reel a month instead.

Is Meta One Advanced worth it for an agency-managed brand?

Look at what Advanced adds over Essential: scheduling, team access without passwords, exportable analytics, insights. For a brand that runs its own social media, that's useful. For a brand managed by an agency, most of it is what the agency's tools already do. Your agency already plans the calendar, gets approvals, publishes and sends a monthly report. Paying Meta ₹2,099 a month per account for a second version of that is hard to justify.

The exception is Stories. Many scheduling tools, ours included, don't publish Instagram Stories through Meta's API, so someone posts them by hand. If a brand lives on Stories, Advanced's Stories scheduling may save the team real time. Do the sum: the hours your team spends posting Stories each month against ₹2,099.

How should an agency set it up for a client?

  • Buy it on the client's account and the client's card. Never on the agency's card. If you part ways, the subscription and the verified identity should stay with the business, just as the account access should never depend on a shared password.
  • Check eligibility first. Turn on two-factor authentication, confirm the business details match the documents the owner will submit, and make sure the name on the Page matches the business. Do this before you mention a price.
  • Write down what was promised. One line in the client's file: "Badge, protection and support. Not a reach boost." It saves an argument in month three.
  • Track the renewal. It's a monthly charge on the client's card. Note the start date and plan in your client onboarding record so nobody is surprised.
  • Don't bill it as a service. Setting it up takes under an hour. If you charge, charge for the setup, not a monthly fee on top of Meta's.

Whether you're an agency in Jaipur handling jewellers or one in a metro handling D2C brands, the pattern is the same: the badge protects the account you're building. The building is still your job.

Frequently asked questions

Does Meta Verified increase reach on Instagram?

Not in the feed or Reels, going by what Meta has announced. Meta's promise for verified businesses is better placement in search results, plus the badge, impersonation protection and account support. Feed and Reels reach still depend on the content itself and on how often you post. Treat the badge as trust and protection, not distribution.

How much does Meta Verified for business cost in India?

Meta launched it in India in July 2024 from ₹639 a month for one app. In September 2026 Meta added Meta One business plans: Essential from ₹639, Advanced from ₹2,099, Expert from ₹6,399 and Max from ₹20,990 a month. Check the price shown in the app before buying, because plans and offers change.

Should an agency buy Meta Verified for a client on the agency's card?

No. Buy it from the client's own account, on the client's own payment method, so the subscription, the verified identity and the support history stay with the business if you part ways. The agency's job is to recommend it, check eligibility, set it up with the client and note the renewal date.
