What does a GST invoice for a marketing agency need?

Digital marketing services in India carry 18% GST. Most agency work, from social media management to SEO and ad management, is billed under SAC 998361 (advertising services). The invoice also needs your GSTIN, the client's GSTIN for B2B, the place of supply and a serial number.

You set your agency's state once and each client's place of supply in their billing details. The invoice then picks the split itself: CGST + SGST when the client is in your state, IGST when they are not, and zero-rated when you mark an export. Each line carries its own HSN/SAC code.

How is TDS handled on agency invoices?

Many business clients deduct TDS before paying you. For most social media retainers that is 1% (individual or HUF agency) or 2% (firm, LLP or company) under the contract-work rules, Section 194C until March 2026 and now Section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. TDS is calculated on the value before GST.

Each invoice has a TDS rate field. It starts at 2% for clients with a GSTIN and you can change it. The invoice shows the net amount you will actually receive, so a ₹23,600 invoice with ₹400 deducted is not chased as ₹400 "short". At month-end you download a TDS register with each client's PAN and GSTIN for your CA to match against your TDS credits. More detail: TDS on digital marketing services.

Clients pay inside their app

Every invoice lands in the client's Payments tab in the client approval app. They open the full invoice and pay through Razorpay by UPI, card, net banking, wallet or EMI, or pay part of it. A partial payment marks the invoice "partial" with the balance shown on both sides. If a client pays by bank transfer, UPI, cash or cheque instead, your team records it against the invoice.

Frequently asked questions

What GST rate applies to digital marketing services?

Digital marketing services such as social media management, SEO, content and ad campaign management are taxed at 18% GST. Most of this work is billed under SAC 998361, advertising services. SAC 998365 applies when you sell internet advertising space or time yourself. Confirm the right code for your work with your CA.

Does it split CGST, SGST and IGST automatically?

Yes. You set your agency's state once and each client's place of supply. When the client is in your state, the invoice shows CGST and SGST at half the rate each. When the client is in another state, it shows IGST at the full rate. Exports can be marked zero-rated.

How is TDS handled on agency invoices?

You enter the TDS rate the client deducts on each invoice, and it is worked out on the value before GST. The invoice shows the net amount you will actually receive. A month-wise TDS register with client PAN and GSTIN can be downloaded for your CA to match against your TDS credits.

Can clients pay GST invoices online?

Yes. Each invoice reaches the client's own app, where they can view it in full and pay through Razorpay by UPI, card, net banking, wallet or EMI, or pay part of the amount. Payments received by bank transfer, UPI, cash or cheque can be recorded by your team.

Can I bill monthly retainers automatically?

Yes. Set up a recurring template per client with the amount and invoice day. With auto-billing switched on, a draft is prepared a couple of days before, ready for you to approve, edit, skip or send. Reminders then follow by email, and by WhatsApp if you connect it.

Does My Digital Sevak generate e-invoices with an IRN?

No. E-invoicing applies once your aggregate turnover crosses ₹5 crore, and the IRN comes from the government's invoice registration portal. My Digital Sevak creates GST tax invoices and GSTR-1 data, but it does not register invoices on the IRP for you.
